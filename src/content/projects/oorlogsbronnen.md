Oorlogsbronnen.nl connects the collections of dozens of Dutch archives, museums, and heritage organizations under the WO2NET network, all built around a shared goal: making WWII history searchable and personal.

## Role

As UX/information designer, I've worked with the WO2NET network since 2021, facilitating workshops with archive partners on how their collections should connect: announcements ("bekendmakingen"), the map module, and oral history recordings.

## Approach

When the platform moved from Drupal to Sanity, I led the redesign of the homepage, article pages, and person pages, working through shared boards with partners to agree on how announcements and life-lines/persons should be structured and displayed. I presented this approach together with colleagues to the wider network.

## Outcome

The partnership has continued for years and remains active today, with new workshops (including one on AI, planned for WO2NET's 2026 network day) still being scheduled. It's one of the clearest examples of a client relationship built on trust: complex, multi-stakeholder decisions made workable through structure.
