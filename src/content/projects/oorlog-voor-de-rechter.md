Oorlog voor de Rechter unlocks the archives of the Centraal Archief Bijzondere Rechtspleging (CABR) — case files from the post-war prosecution of collaboration — for both researchers and the public. It's a collaboration between the Nationaal Archief, NIOD, WO2NET, and data-search partner Spinque.

## Role

I facilitated the project's kickoff with the Nationaal Archief, Huygens Institute, NIOD, WO2NET, and Spinque, and led the design of how people search, browse, and navigate through dossiers and legal cases.

## Approach

Working from regular reviews with the consortium, I designed the search results and case/dossier navigation, always with the underlying tension in mind: this is sensitive personal data, only partially public since 2025, but the historical and public interest in accessing it is significant.

## Outcome

Internally, this was one of the two projects (alongside Spacebuzz) that challenged me the most in 2024 — precisely because the stakes, privacy versus openness, were real, not hypothetical.
