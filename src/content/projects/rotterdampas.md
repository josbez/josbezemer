## The Challenge

Rotterdampas has run for over a decade and serves tens of thousands of Rotterdam residents. The challenge of a project this long isn't a single redesign, it's keeping a public product coherent, accessible, and trustworthy while its app, website, chatbot, and backend all keep evolving separately.

## My Role

I've been product owner / information designer across the full product since 2020: the app, the website, a chatbot (RPAS), accessibility, analytics, and the webshop, in a partnership that passed its 10-year mark in 2024.

## Approach

The highest-leverage work here was rarely visible: designing the chatbot's role-and-goal behaviour, setting up analytics that could actually answer usage questions, publishing a formal accessibility statement. None of that is a feature launch, but all of it is what makes a public product still trustworthy after ten years.

## Impact

Rotterdampas has become a reference case, used in internal training and cited as inspiration in accessibility advice for other cultural clients, and it remains one of the longest, most consistent client relationships in my portfolio.

## Learnings

Longevity is its own kind of design problem. The skill isn't shipping the next feature, it's making decisions today that won't need to be undone in two years.
