Rotterdampas is the city of Rotterdam's pass for culture, sport, and leisure discounts, used by tens of thousands of residents. I've worked on it since 2020, across BS&F and the gemeente, in a partnership that passed its 10-year mark in 2024.

## Role

Long-term product owner / information designer across the full product: the app, the website, a chatbot (RPAS), accessibility, analytics, and the webshop.

## Approach

Beyond feature work, this case includes designing the chatbot's role-and-goal prompt behaviour, setting up analytics to actually understand usage, and publishing a formal accessibility statement — the unglamorous infrastructure that makes a public product trustworthy at scale.

## Outcome

Rotterdampas has become a reference case: used in internal training, cited as inspiration in accessibility advice for other cultural clients, and one of the longest, most consistent client relationships in my portfolio.
