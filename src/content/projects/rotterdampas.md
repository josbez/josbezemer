## The Challenge

Rotterdampas has run for over a decade and serves tens of thousands of Rotterdam residents. The challenge of a project this long isn't a single redesign, it's keeping a public product coherent, accessible, and trustworthy while its app, website, chatbot, and backend all keep evolving separately.

## My Role

I've been product owner / information designer across the full product since a five-day design sprint in January 2020 set the direction, app first, that the following six years of work were built on: the app, the website, a chatbot (RPAS), accessibility, analytics, and the webshop, in a partnership that passed its 10-year mark in 2024.

## Approach

That first sprint gave the redesign its problem statement and its answer: make the app the primary channel, not an accessory to the website. I backed that with two full rounds of user research that year, a baseline in May and a usability test in July that deliberately reused several of the same respondents so I could measure change rather than capture a single snapshot, and used the results to write the app's complete functional backlog and its measurement plan from scratch. The years after that were about extending that foundation without breaking it: a full account-management area, a notification system rolled out as a four-phase roadmap instead of one feature, a new website built from an explicit interaction concept before a single screen was drawn, and two payment integrations, online spending and voucher redemption, that each ran across three to five external technical partners at once. One of the least visible but highest-leverage fixes: I traced a recurring data bug back to a structural mismatch between the middleware's calendar year and the pass's own March-to-February year, and collapsed it into a single configurable value instead of patching each symptom separately. Accessibility became a standing thread rather than a one-off exercise: a dedicated WCAG round on contrast and screen-reader behaviour in 2022, a structured audit with accessibility.nl in 2023, a published accessibility statement, and a review process I set up for handling app-store feedback across the studio's other public-sector apps.

## Impact

Rotterdampas has become a reference case, used in internal training and cited as inspiration in accessibility advice for other cultural clients. The app has carried a 4.5+ star rating across both app stores for years, was consistently the most-used channel among all age groups, for both inspiration and everyday transactions, and was nominated for a Dutch Interactive Award in the Tools category in 2023. It remains one of the longest, most consistent client relationships in my portfolio.

## Learnings

Longevity is its own kind of design problem. The skill isn't shipping the next feature, it's making decisions today that won't need to be undone in two years.
