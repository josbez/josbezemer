Spacebuzz brings a VR spaceflight experience into primary schools worldwide, paired with a supporting lesson programme. My work focused on the system behind that programme: the CMS that holds and structures the lessons.

## Role

As interaction designer, I owned the high-level information architecture for the CMS and lesson-module structure, working closely with Spacebuzz to translate their pedagogical model into something a content team could actually manage.

## Approach

Starting from the kickoff in February 2024, I worked with the Spacebuzz team to break down the 12-lesson methodology into "educational units" that could be adapted per country and region, without duplicating structure for every market.

## Outcome

Spacebuzz was one of my two main projects of 2024, and one that pushed me outside familiar territory: less about a single audience or platform, more about designing a structure flexible enough to hold a growing, international programme.
