## The Challenge

Spacebuzz's 12-lesson space-education programme worked well for one country. Scaling it internationally meant the underlying CMS couldn't just repeat that structure per market, it needed a model flexible enough to hold different languages, curricula, and regional partners without turning into a maintenance burden.

## My Role

I owned the high-level information architecture for the CMS and lesson-module structure, translating Spacebuzz's pedagogical model, worked out together with the Spacebuzz team, into something a content team could actually manage day to day.

## Approach

Rather than designing "a CMS," I designed the unit of reuse: breaking the 12-lesson methodology into "educational units" that could be adapted per country and region without duplicating structure for every new market. That decision is what made scaling a matter of configuration, not re-engineering.

## Impact

Spacebuzz was one of my two main projects of 2024. The real outcome is structural: a programme that can grow into new countries without the CMS needing to be redesigned each time.

## Learnings

International scale isn't a bigger version of the same problem, it's a different problem: the job shifts from designing content to designing the rules content has to follow.
