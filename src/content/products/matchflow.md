## Follow the match, even when you can’t watch. {#hero}

Matchflow puts the match you care about in the macOS menu bar: the score, and a bar that shows who is on top. One click opens the flow of the game, every goal and card, and the live table.

- [Download the beta](https://github.com/josbez/matchflow/releases/latest/download/Matchflow.dmg)
- [How to install](#install)

Free · Beta 0.4 · macOS 15 Sequoia or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

## Not just the score. Who’s on top. {#states}

A 1–1 can mean your club is pinned back, or pushing for the winner. The bar under the score fills with the home colour up to the home side’s share of the momentum over the last ten minutes. One glance tells you which way the match is going. When the momentum turns for ten minutes, a small flame appears next to the side that took over.

## One click deeper. {#features}

### The flow of the match

Momentum minute by minute, bars up for one side and down for the other, then the race for expected goals. One line sums it up: “Feyenoord pressing · 50% possession in the second half”.

### Every goal and card

In order, under the score, with the minute. During a VAR check the goal waits, so you never celebrate one that doesn’t count.

### Now live

The live table as it stands right now, with what each club is playing and who moves up or down. Plus everything else that’s live, the European cups included.

### Last five

Your club’s last five results right under the match card. Click one to see that match again, with line-ups, stats and highlights when there are any.

## Follow what you care about. {#follow}

Follow clubs, countries and whole competitions, each in your own order. With several matches on at once, the menu bar shows what is live first, then what matters most to you. It never switches while you are looking.

### Clubs

Your club, and the one your friend supports.

### Countries

Your national team, in the Nations League and at the World Cup.

### Competitions

A whole league. Matchflow picks its most exciting live match and stays with it until full time.

## Line-ups and stats, live. {#lineups}

Both teams on one pitch with live ratings, goals and cards beside each player, and who came on for whom. Click a player for their match so far. Or open the stats: possession, expected goals, shots and where the attacks come from, for the whole match or per half, with the better side marked.

## On your desktop, too. {#widgets}

Widgets in three sizes: the score, the bar and the flow, from a quick look to the live table. They update on a goal, a red card or a change in status, and say so when the numbers are old.

## Notifications on your terms. {#notifications}

Pick the moments you want to hear about, one by one. Never twice, and never for a goal still under VAR review. In a meeting? Matchflow checks your calendar, holds everything, and sends one summary when you’re done.

- Goals
- Red cards
- Line-up known

## Light, warm and calm. {#calm}

Matchflow sits in your menu bar all day, so it behaves like it. Only real changes move: the bar slides, the score rolls on a goal. Nothing blinks or pulses, and with Reduce Motion on, nothing moves at all. Light and dark follow your Mac.

## Install in a minute. {#install}

1. [Download the beta](https://github.com/josbez/matchflow/releases/latest/download/Matchflow.dmg) and open the disk image.
2. Drag **Matchflow** to **Applications**.
3. Open Terminal and run: `bash /Volumes/Matchflow/install.sh`
4. Choose what to follow: a club, a country or a competition. The next match appears in the menu bar.

Needs macOS 15 Sequoia or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.

## Questions. {#faq}

### Is it free?

Yes. Matchflow is free. It is in beta: it works, and it gets better with every match day. Updates arrive in the app.

### Which matches can I follow?

Clubs, countries and whole competitions: the Eredivisie, Premier League, LaLiga, Bundesliga, Serie A and Ligue 1, and national teams in the Nations League and the World Cup. With several matches live, the menu bar shows what is live first, then follows your own order. Click any other live match in the popover to follow it for the rest of the round.

### Why are some parts missing for some matches?

Not every match has the same depth of live data. Matchflow only shows what is actually there: when momentum, ratings or possession are missing, that part disappears instead of being guessed.

### Why do I need to run a Terminal command?

The app is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch. The install script removes that block and makes Matchflow start at login.

### Can I open it from the keyboard?

Yes. ⌃⌥M opens and closes the popover from any app, and you can pick another shortcut in settings. Inside the popover, ⌘1 to ⌘4 switch pages.

### How does it update?

When there is a new version, the gear in the popover gets a dot. Open settings and click Update. Every update is signed, and the app refuses one without a valid signature.

### Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. A native iPhone app, with the live score on your lock screen, is the next step.

### Is this an official app of a club or league?

No. Matchflow is an independent project, not affiliated with any club, league or data provider.
